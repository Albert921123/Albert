# Construction-industry anchor and extended-reading policy

## Formal admission gate for the first eight sections

The following rows are business views of the construction ecosystem, not unrestricted general-news buckets: `fintech`, `sourcing`, `matching`, `employment`, `overseas`, `leadership`, `enterprise`, and `capital`.

Discovery may begin with broad terminology to avoid missing candidates. A candidate may enter reader-facing HTML only when its original page contains both:

1. a qualifying row event; and
2. an explicit construction anchor in at least one of these forms: `construction`, `building-materials`, `real-estate`, `infrastructure`, `engineering-services`, `construction-equipment`, `project-owner-or-contractor`, or `engineering-supply-chain`.

Record the selected value as `industry_anchor_type` and quote or paraphrase the page evidence in `industry_anchor_evidence`. Generic claims such as “有产业价值” or an inferred multi-hop relationship do not qualify.

### Row boundaries

- `fintech` 数科: digital procurement/trading, construction supply-chain finance, factoring, settlement, leasing, project-fund supervision, trusted data/data assets, and operating analytics used by an AEC/property/infrastructure/materials actor. Exclude generic bank apps, consumer finance, generic AI and bank IT with no construction customer or object.
- `sourcing` 寻源: a verifiable procurement need, supplier call, framework agreement or purchasing signal for a construction project or construction supply chain.
- `matching` 撮合: a verifiable supply-demand connection, cooperation, signing or joint solution involving construction-ecosystem actors or delivery objects.
- `employment` 用工: construction labor, professional qualification, project/digital-engineering roles, wage/social-security/safety or skills demand.
- `overseas` 海外: international projects, local operations, export, overseas factories, market access, compliance or cross-border capital involving contractors, developers, materials, equipment or infrastructure.
- `leadership` 高管观点: a named speaker's fresh view tied to construction, real estate, infrastructure, building materials, engineering technology/software or the sector's industrial finance.
- `enterprise` 企业经营: orders, revenue, cash collection, backlog, organization, strategy or risk of a construction-ecosystem company.
- `capital` 投融资: equity, debt, funds, M&A, REITs, project finance or capex tied to a construction-ecosystem company or asset.

## Extended reading: five mandatory discovery tracks

The `extended` row is not a leftovers bucket. Its profile and evidence must cover all five tracks before the row can close:

1. policy and macro interpretation;
2. industry research reports;
3. enterprise or project cases;
4. technology or business-model observations;
5. trend and risk专题.

Prefer the original publisher, government/research institution, recognized industry association, leading national/financial media, established construction/property/materials vertical media, or an attributable public research report. Every retained item must be substantive and explicitly anchored to construction, building materials, real estate, infrastructure or engineering supply chains.
