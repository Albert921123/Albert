# Broad discovery and evidence source ladder

Read this reference for live generation runs with web search. It broadens candidate discovery without weakening event verification. Domains and publishers are examples of maintained source families, not endorsements and not a fixed whitelist.

## Separate discovery from evidence

Use four layers in parallel:

1. **Original public records:** central/local government, regulators, exchanges, procurement platforms, standards publishers, company IR/newsrooms and event counterparties.
2. **National and statutory media:** 新华网、人民网、证券时报、中国证券报、上海证券报、财联社、21财经、第一财经、中国建设报/住建部转载、工人日报、中国对外承包工程商会 and comparable authoritative publishers.
3. **Established vertical media:** 中国电子银行网、观点网、我的钢铁网、建筑时报/建设行业媒体、北极星、索比光伏、国际储能网、行业协会媒体、国际工程媒体 and comparable sector publishers that identify actors, records and dates.
4. **Discovery mirrors and major portals:** 东方财富公告/数据中心、同花顺行业频道、中国证券网公告页、腾讯新闻、网易、新浪财经 and comparable stable reprints. Use these to find leads and, for ordinary events only, as clearly labeled T2 evidence when the underlying filing, government document, company announcement or original publisher is named and the original route is unavailable.

## Minimum mixed-source discovery

For a normal live run, use at least two independent lanes for every selected 7–10 relevance or high-output row before declaring the row ready to render: one primary/statutory channel and one national, major-financial, vertical, procurement, exchange/disclosure or attributable-mirror channel. The second lane is mandatory even when the first official channel already yields one valid item. Open and compare up to five fresh plausible candidates across the lanes when available; record why strong non-duplicate candidates were not selected. A prior daily HTML or its ledger is not one of these lanes and cannot supply a reader-facing card without a new in-window verification.

Use the following concrete pairings where applicable:

- 政府宏观、标准规范、绿色低碳、城市更新、专项债：中央/部委或省级主管部门 + 地方政府、公共资源/项目执行或权威媒体。
- 企业经营、投融资、数科、光伏、半导体：公司/交易所/法定披露 + 证券时报、中国证券报、上证报、财联社、东方财富/同花顺公告镜像或专业垂直媒体。
- 寻源、用工、建筑科技、建筑软件：全国或地方公共资源/采购、企业或产品官方渠道 + 行业协会、建设行业媒体或技术垂直媒体。
- 海外、海运：项目业主/承包商/港口航运机构 + 国际工程、航运或全国财经媒体。

For a daily brief intended for leadership, use the channels above as a **source reservoir**, not merely a fallback list. A high-output row that only checks official sites and yields one generic headline has not completed mixed-source discovery. Conversely, do not treat portal copies as interchangeable: prefer the original government, filing, company or named publisher where accessible, and keep the closest accessible established reporting page when it contributes project scale, transaction terms, market reaction, or other facts absent from the original.

The second lane is not a lower-quality filler: it is a discovery and resilience route. Final cards still need the event, actor, date and direct URL, and retain the appropriate T1/T2 label.

Search results and anonymous content farms are never final evidence. A familiar brand does not override missing dates, unclear actors or unsupported claims.

## Required breadth for large subscriptions

For subscriptions with 12 or more sections:

- Give every row its field and source/actor passes before deep reading.
- For every high-output row with fewer than two main stories, add one layer-2 search and one independent layer-3 or layer-4 search.
- Search central and local channels separately for government, standards, green, employment and urban renewal.
- Search official disclosure records and at least one financial-media/disclosure-mirror family separately for enterprise, capital, fintech, photovoltaic, storage and semiconductor.
- Search Chinese and international/project-owner channels separately for overseas.
- For broad rows, the field, actor and official-record passes must omit construction/AEC terms. Run the business-intersection pass afterwards and use it for ranking, not admission.
- For dynamically entered custom interests, expand the exact phrase into actors, event verbs, regulators/registries, products, transactions and policy terms before searching. Do not require the label to exist in a predefined table.
- In addition, give every high-output or 7–10 row one named relevant Chinese discovery publisher from Layer 2, 3 or 4. A run cannot claim its source pool was broad if all completed rows were discovered solely from government or company homepages.

## Common query forms

Combine the run date/window with exact section cues and event verbs:

- official/actor: `site:<official-domain> <section cue> 公告|发布|签约|中标|业绩`
- national/financial media: `<section cue> 8月25日 公告|政策|项目|投产|并购|报告`
- disclosure mirror: `<company/section cue> 公告 半年度报告 投资者关系 中标`
- local government: `site:*.gov.cn <section cue> 通知|公示|行动方案|项目清单`
- vertical media: `<section cue> 项目|价格|容量|平台|融资|合作 <run date>`

Do not require literal construction keywords in broad fields. Add the AEC/business-intersection query only after the field and actor passes.

## Balanced scope expansion

Use the three-level admission policy from `topics.md`:

- retain fresh A-level direct field events;
- retain B-level customer, supplier, capital, regulatory and infrastructure events with a clear business bridge;
- when no A/B event exists, retain at most one decision-useful C-level macro-impact event;
- exclude D-level weak associations.

For an active high-output row that still has fewer than two full cards, use both a national/statutory discovery source and an independent vertical or disclosure mirror, opening up to five additional candidates. A compact related card does not cancel this expansion pass.

## Evidence labels

- `T1一手`: official government/regulator/exchange/procurement/standards/company/event-counterparty page.
- `T1法定披露`: statutory disclosure media or filing system carrying the original document.
- `T2权威转述`: established national, financial or vertical publisher that identifies the underlying event and date.
- `T2公告镜像`: stable mirror reproducing or clearly pointing to a named filing or announcement.
- `T2转载`: major portal carrying an identified original publisher; use only when no closer accessible route exists.

Never upgrade a T2 page to T1 merely because its story is accurate. Preserve the label in the story card and retrieval audit.
